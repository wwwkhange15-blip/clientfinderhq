How to find freelance clients with no portfolio

Most beginners wait until they feel ready. Clients don't care about readiness. They care about proof that you can solve their problem. Here is a 30-day plan to get that proof and your first reply.

1. Make three sample projects

You don't need paying clients to build a portfolio. Pick three real businesses you know, such as a local shop or a small YouTube channel, and do a short piece of work for each: a rewritten homepage, a sample logo, a short video edit. Label them clearly as sample work. Three good samples beat ten weak ones.

2. Pick one service and one type of client

"I do everything" gets ignored. "I design simple landing pages for fitness coaches" gets remembered. Choose one service and one kind of client, then change it later if it isn't working.

3. Put everything on one page

Your profile page only needs four things: what you do, who it's for, your three samples, and how to contact you. A free page on any platform is enough to start.

4. Send 10 personal messages a day

This is where clients come from. Find 10 businesses that clearly need your service, look at one specific thing they could improve, and tell them. Keep it short. Our cold email guide covers this in more detail.

Hi [Name], I noticed [specific thing on their site or page]. I help [type of client] with [your service], and I made a quick sample of how this could look. Would you like me to send it over?

Don't paste the same message to everyone. One specific detail in the first line makes the difference between a reply and silence.

5. Follow up once or twice

Most people don't answer the first message because they're busy, not because they said no. Wait three to four days, then send a two-line reminder. Stop after the second follow-up.

Mistakes that cost you replies

Writing long messages about yourself, offering many services at once, and sending messages with no personal detail are the most common. Also avoid promising results you can't guarantee, such as "double your sales".

What to do this week

Choose your one service, finish the first sample, and send your first 10 messages. Track who you contacted and who replied in a simple spreadsheet. After 30 days, you'll know what's working.
